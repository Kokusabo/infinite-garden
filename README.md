# infinite garden

a garden on a tape. it was recorded over and over, and nobody remembers what was on it first.
every plot is grown from a number. the next number grows something else. there is no last plot.

scroll or swipe for another plot. touch for another green. hold to see the letters. type, and things grow.

---

made of code: every leaf, blade and glint is painted by the page itself. no photographs.
fonts: VT323 and IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `fonts/`).
